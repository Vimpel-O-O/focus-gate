01 Choose your direction

A broad topic filter. Related discussions, explainers and news are welcome—you don’t need an immediate task.

02 Connect your local service

Your OpenAI API key stays in the local service. Do not paste it here.

Changes apply when you reload an open YouTube tab.

What gets checked

Video title, channel, description and captions when available—not the footage itself. Your goals and the retrieved text are sent to the OpenAI API. Unrelated videos, uncertain decisions and failed checks stay paused. You choose videos on YouTube as usual; this tool only reviews playback.

This is a personal attention aid. It can be disabled, and it does not block other browsers or downloaded videos. AI can misclassify content.
